## 01. Nexus
**AI Research Platform for Students**

### Overview
One question, one verified answer. Nexus runs multiple AI models in parallel, cross-checks their responses, and surfaces only the most accurate, agreed-upon information. Students ask once and get the answer backed by consensus, no tab hopping, no conflicting responses, no uncertainty.

### Project Details
| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Role** | UX/UI Designer |
| **Duration** | 10 weeks |
| **Tools** | Figma, Cursor, Lovable |
| **Team** | Internship Project |
| **Platform** | Web Application |

### Key Features
- **Scanning for Better Answers** - The strongest response wins
- **Homework Intelligence** - Questions matched to best AI models per subject
- **6x Faster** - Multiple models run in parallel
- **Single Source** - Ask once, get the best answer across all models
- **Smarter Studying** - Verified answers, clearer explanations
- **Seamless Integrations** - Works with your favorite tools

### Key Stats
- 45% decrease in study time
- 97% code accuracy
- Trusted by students from MIT, Yale, UC Berkeley, Georgia Tech, UPenn

## 04. Spotify Redesign
**Listening Threads — Intent-Based Discovery**

### Overview
Reimagining music discovery through intention, memory, and personal meaning. A concept redesign exploring how Spotify could help users rediscover their relationship with music. Through user research and affinity mapping, I identified key pain points around passive listening and designed features like Listening Memory, Threads, and Emotional Clusters to bring intention back to music discovery.

### Project Details
| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Role** | UX Designer |
| **Duration** | 2 weeks |
| **Tools** | Figma, Cursor, Lovable |
| **Platform** | Mobile (iOS) |

### Problem Statement
How might we transform music streaming from **passive consumption** into **active reflection**, helping listeners understand their emotional relationship with music while still enabling **effortless discovery**?

### Key Features
- **Listening Threads** — Finite, behavior-driven collections with clear explanations
- **Why This Exists** — Every recommendation comes with a human-readable explanation
- **Listening Memory** — Visual patterns of your emotional listening over time
- **Emotional Clusters** — Group songs by feeling, not just genre
- **Rediscovery Moments** — Resurface forgotten favorites with personal context

### Key Screens
- Your Threads (Home)
- Listening Memory Insights
- Emotional Clusters
- Thread Card with Tracks
- Thread Detail View
- Now Playing Memory

### Research Insights
- Survey sample: 24 Spotify users
- 72% of listening is background noise
- 78% forget songs they've saved
- 65% find recommendations opaque
- 87% of test users preferred Threads over current discovery
- 4.6/5 average rating for Listening Memory feature

- **Case Study:** `/case-study/spotify`

## 05. HABITat
**Gamified Habit Tracking for College Students**

🏆 **Awarded Most User-Centered Design**

### Overview
Track your habit, build your habitat. Most habit tracking apps fail because they lack incentives, rewards, and accountability. HABITat reimagines habit tracking through gamification. As you maintain your habits, you build a virtual habitat and unlock adorable animal companions. The app combines reward systems with social accountability, making habit-building feel less like a chore and more like nurturing your own little world.

### Project Details
| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Role** | UX Designer |
| **Duration** | 4-Week Sprint |
| **Tools** | Figma, FigJam |
| **Team** | 4 Designers |
| **Platform** | Mobile App |

### Problem Statement
How might we design an app for **college students** that tracks their **habits** in a clear and concise way while keeping them **engaged and accountable**?

### Key Features
- Gamification through virtual habitat building
- Collectible animal companions unlocked by maintaining streaks
- Social accountability with friends
- Reward systems for consistency
